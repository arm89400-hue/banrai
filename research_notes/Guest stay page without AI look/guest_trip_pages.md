# Post-booking "your trip / your stay" guest pages: what real hospitality brands show, and how

Scope note: these notes come from about 15 web searches and fetches. Brand-specific UI layouts for Booking.com, Hotel Tonight, Mr & Mrs Smith, Design Hotels, Aman and Hoshinoya were **not** documented in any public source I could reach (they sit behind logins and emails). Most of the solid material is from Airbnb's own help and newsroom pages, guidebook vendors (Touch Stay, Hostfully, Duve), Six Senses job postings and press, and real farm and glamping arrival sheets. Vendor figures are self-reported.

## 1. What do Airbnb, Booking.com, boutique/luxury brands and small farm stays show after booking, and in what order?

### Takeaway
Airbnb is the only big brand with a publicly documented structure. It puts the trip in an itinerary or timeline (2025 Trips tab), and the check-in details unlock about 48 hours before arrival in a fixed order: check-in, directions, check-in method, Wi-Fi, house manual, house rules, check-out, guidebook. Luxury brands (Six Senses) put the effort into pre-arrival *preparation* (questionnaires, packing tips, highlighted activities) more than into page layout. Small farm and glamping stays lead with practical arrival facts: road, vehicle, signal, supplies, and text the host when you arrive.

### Cited Findings
- Airbnb: the check-in details for a reservation sit on the Trips page. On desktop the path is Trips > reservation > "Checking in" > "How to get inside". The host's instructions arrive 48 hours (local time) before the stay starts. They typically include the exact address and directions, how to get in, parking, the Wi-Fi password, house rules and host contact. Airbnb advises saving them offline. — [Airbnb Help article 41](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/41)
- Airbnb: if the stay is under 48 hours away and the info is missing, the guest is told to message the host. Some regional pages label the section "Things to know". — [Airbnb Help article 41](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/41); [Airbnb IE](https://www.airbnb.ie/help/article/100)
- Airbnb 2025 Summer Release: the new Trips tab is "an advanced travel itinerary with a schedule of your trip, details about your home, and the services and experiences you've booked". The announcement image shows a Mexico City itinerary where check-in is one timeline entry next to a booked show. Messaging is a separate, revamped tab with photo and video sharing. — [Airbnb Newsroom, 2025 Summer Release](https://news.airbnb.com/product-releases/airbnb-2025-summer-release/)
- Airbnb Arrival Guide sections, in the order listed: Check-in, Directions, Check-in method, Wi-Fi details, House manual, House rules, Check-out instructions, Guidebooks, Interaction preferences. Each section has a strict 500-character limit and allows no photos, videos, QR codes or subcategories. — [Touch Stay, The Airbnb Arrival Guide](https://touchstay.com/blog/airbnb-arrival-guide)
- Booking.com: I found no official description of the manage-booking layout. Third-party guides list the post-booking actions as change dates (if the policy allows), cancel, and contact the property. The payment model can be pay at the property, prepay online, or a partial deposit. — [Holidu help](https://www.holidu.co.uk/help/guest/placing-a-booking/how-can-i-make-my-reservation-on-bookingcom) (secondary; treat with caution)
- A generic confirmation usually carries the property address, total cost, payment breakdown, check-in and check-out times, and the cancellation policy. — [Lodgify encyclopedia](https://encyclopedia.lodgify.com/booking-confirmation)
- Six Senses: after booking, guests receive packing tips and highlighted activities "so they can begin to think about their stay". — [Spafinder](https://www.spafinder.com/blog/spa/six-senses-service-sparks-return-visits/)
- Six Senses "Sleep With Six Senses" starts with an online pre-arrival questionnaire. A Sleep Ambassador uses it to set up the room before arrival. — [eHotelier](https://insights.ehotelier.com/global-news/2016/09/21/six-senses-launches-new-enhanced-sleep-standard/) (2016, older than the preferred range)
- Six Senses Guest Experience Maker (GEM) duties: review guest profiles and history before arrival, brief departments on guest preferences, and prepare arrival amenities including a **welcome letter**. — [Six Senses Ibiza GEM job posting](https://sixsensesibiza.teamtailor.com/jobs/6096443-guest-experience-maker-manager-six-senses-ibiza)
- Mr & Mrs Smith: the public pages only show "Smith Extra" arrival perks (for example a glass of wine each on arrival, or a 10-minute head massage for two) and the Smith24 travel-specialist team. The confirmation and trip-page layout is not public. — [Mr & Mrs Smith offer, Harmon Guest House](https://www.mrandmrssmith.com/luxury-hotels/harmon-guest-house/offers); [Wallawwa offer](https://www.mrandmrssmith.com/luxury-hotels/wallawwa/offers)
- Hoshinoya Kyoto: the signature arrival is a free private boat of about 15 minutes up the Oi River. It runs on a schedule, so guests must coordinate their arrival time. A guest who misses it calls for a car or takes a taxi to the service entrance. This comes from a third-party listing with inconsistent prices, so treat it as unverified. — [Roomer Travel listing](https://www.roomertravel.com/ca/featured/hoshinoya-kyoto.h705703)
- NZ glamping info sheet (Canopy Camping, Camp Kekerengu): step-by-step turn directions, the host's phone number, and a warning about a steep track ("if you have a 2WD, please let us know in advance so that we can be on-hand to assist you"). A later version adds "Please text Pip when you have arrived and she will meet you there." — [Canopy Camping info sheet v3](https://canopycamping.co.nz/sites/default/files/2018-11/CAMP%20KEKERENGU%20INFO%20SHEET3.pdf); [v4](https://canopycamping.co.nz/sites/default/files/2019-09/CAMP%20KEKERENGU%20INFO%20SHEETv4.pdf)
- US farm (Harvest Hosts): the stop point (the farm store with a turnaround driveway) is kept separate from parking, and a check-in window is given (12:00 PM - 8:00 PM ET). — [Harvest Hosts listing](https://harvesthosts.com/hosts/new-york/gLiiKXbXPiZpPrXZmUtY)
- South African farm stay: guests were warned about a 38 km gravel road, with advice on tyre pressure and speed. Reviews also flag zero cell reception and no local takeaway (buy supplies in town first). — [Kayak, Waterval Farmstay](https://www.kayak.com/Graaff-Reinet-Hotels-Waterval-Farmstay.2301197.ksp)

### Inferences
- A workable content order for a small farmstay page, taken from Airbnb's order plus the farm sheets: (1) dates, nights and booking status; (2) how to get there, including road conditions and the last shop or fuel stop; (3) arrival: check-in window, where to stop and park, "send us a message when you reach the gate"; (4) money still owed; (5) message the host; (6) things to do on the farm and nearby; (7) house rules and check-out.
- Airbnb holds the access details back until 48 hours before arrival. A homestay with a staffed welcome has no secrets to hold back, so it can show everything at once and simply put "arrival" first as the date gets close.
- The 2025 Airbnb move to a timeline (check-in as one event among others) supports an itinerary or day-by-day format over a dashboard of tiles.

### Gaps
- No public documentation of the post-booking pages of Booking.com, Hotel Tonight, Design Hotels, Aman or Hoshinoya. Finding them would need real confirmation emails or app screenshots (for example Mobbin, which is behind a login).
- No verified Hoshinoya pre-arrival email content.

## 2. How do they present dates and countdown, directions and map, check-in instructions, balance due, host messaging, things to do, and house rules?

### Takeaway
The patterns that recur: dates as a schedule or timeline entry; directions as written turn-by-turn steps plus photos (where to park, the gate), not just a map pin; check-in as numbered steps with a time window; messaging as a clear separate channel; house rules kept short. Visuals (photos of the parking spot, a short video of the lock) are what guidebook vendors push hardest. There is no public pattern for a "balance due" display on a guest stay page.

### Cited Findings
- Airbnb tells guests to save check-in instructions offline in case the connection drops while travelling. — [Airbnb Help article 41](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/41)
- Touch Stay: visuals "are useful for reinforcing written information" and are "essential for neurodiverse guests". Its examples are a key-lock video and photos of where to park. — [Touch Stay, Airbnb Arrival Guide](https://touchstay.com/blog/airbnb-arrival-guide)
- Touch Stay guidebooks are organised by stage (Pre-Stay, Arrival, Mid-Stay, Post-Stay), and hosts can control when each piece of content appears relative to the stay dates. — [Touch Stay digital guidebook](https://touchstay.com/digital-guidebook); [Touch Stay FAQs](https://touchstay.com/blog/popular-faqs-to-include-in-your-digital-guest-welcome-book)
- Typical arrival-stage questions are about check-in, finding your way around the property, and nearby amenities. Touch Stay recommends step-by-step check-in instructions and an explanation of early check-in options. — [Touch Stay FAQs](https://touchstay.com/blog/popular-faqs-to-include-in-your-digital-guest-welcome-book)
- Hostfully's list of guidebook contents: check-in instructions, house manual, Wi-Fi, local recommendations, check-out steps. A practitioner says to put the Wi-Fi name and password first, "literally the first thing every guest looks for" (opinion, not data). — [Hostfully blog](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/what-is-a-digital-guidebook); [STR Specialist forum](https://strspecialist.com/community/thread/airbnb-digital-guidebook-best-tools-what-include)
- Booking.com and similar sites support pay at the property, prepay online, or a partial deposit. Any outstanding balance is collected by the property directly. — [Holidu help](https://www.holidu.co.uk/help/guest/placing-a-booking/how-can-i-make-my-reservation-on-bookingcom); [Nightsbridge](https://nightsbridge.screenstepslive.com/a/1535078-booking-com-how-the-connection-works)
- Common pre-arrival email timing advice: 1-2 weeks before arrival, then a reminder 3-5 days before, or one email 2 days before. The content is directions, check-in procedure and booking details (confirmation number, dates, room type, number of guests). — [GuestCentric blog](https://blog.guestcentric.com/5-powerful-ideas-to-create-a-click-worthy-pre-stay-e-mail/)
- Glamping and farm sheets tell guests up front about vehicle needs, the host contact on arrival, and no signal: "zero cell reception at the farm… inform others". — [Canopy Camping](https://canopycamping.co.nz/sites/default/files/2019-09/CAMP%20KEKERENGU%20INFO%20SHEETv4.pdf); [Kayak, Waterval Farmstay](https://www.kayak.com/Graaff-Reinet-Hotels-Waterval-Farmstay.2301197.ksp)

### Inferences
- For a rural Thai farmstay, a photo of the gate or turn-off with one line ("turn left at the blue sign after the 7-Eleven") likely does more than an embedded map. Use a map link for navigation and photos plus words for the last kilometre.
- The balance due should be stated plainly with the amount, the deadline and how to pay (online now, or cash or transfer at the farm). No source shows a luxury pattern for this, so plain clarity is the standard.
- A countdown timer is not documented as a pattern in any source found. "In 12 days" next to the dates is the most that is supported.

### Gaps
- No source gives measured data on which guidebook sections guests open most (Touch Stay is said to have section analytics, but I found no published figures).
- No documented hotel pattern for showing a remaining balance on a guest page.

## 3. Which visual treatments make these pages feel personal and hand-made rather than templated?

### Takeaway
There is little public, citable evidence. What exists points to a **welcome letter** signed by a named person (Six Senses GEM duty), real photos of the property's own arrival points, written directions in the host's own voice (the "text Pip when you arrive" style), and itinerary or timeline structure (Airbnb). Templates sold to hotels (gold letterheads, "Dear Guest", a handwritten-style signature line) show what "templated" looks like.

### Cited Findings
- Six Senses arrival amenities include a welcome letter, with cold towels and welcome drinks. — [Six Senses Ibiza GEM posting](https://sixsensesibiza.teamtailor.com/jobs/6096443-guest-experience-maker-manager-six-senses-ibiza)
- The NZ glamping sheet uses a named host and first-person, specific instructions ("Please text Pip when you have arrived and she will meet you there"). — [Canopy Camping v4](https://canopycamping.co.nz/sites/default/files/2019-09/CAMP%20KEKERENGU%20INFO%20SHEETv4.pdf)
- An example of the templated look: a boutique-hotel letterhead template with a gold header, a "Dear Guest" body and a handwritten-style manager signature line. — [PlatoForms template](https://templates.platoforms.com/pdf/boutique-hotel-guest-services-letterhead)
- Touch Stay recommends personalising the welcome by mentioning something from the guest's booking and giving local tips that match their interests. — [Touch Stay](https://touchstay.com/blog/best-guest-communication-tactics-between-booking-and-arrival)
- Airbnb's 2025 Trips tab uses an itinerary or schedule format that mixes the stay with booked experiences. — [Airbnb Newsroom](https://news.airbnb.com/product-releases/airbnb-2025-summer-release/)
- Beaumier hotels (Canary case study): their earlier pre-arrival check-in was "a static web experience that felt disconnected from the premium service guests expected". — [HotelTechReport, Beaumier](https://hoteltechreport.com/fr/success-stories/operations/canary/beaumier)

### Inferences
- Personal comes from specifics: the host's real name and photo, the farm's own photos (gate, parking, house), the guest's own name and dates, and what will actually be ripe or happening during their dates. Gold borders and script fonts are not what makes it personal.
- A short note signed by the owner at the top, with the practical facts below, matches both the luxury welcome-letter habit and the small-host voice.
- Postcard or ticket metaphors and illustrated maps: no source found that documents these on real guest pages. They are unverified ideas and carry a risk of looking gimmicky or templated.

### Gaps
- No Behance, Dribbble or Mobbin case studies of boutique guest portals came up in search, and Mobbin needs a login.
- No source on editorial typography in guest stay pages at Aman, Design Hotels or Hoshinoya.

## 4. What does research say reduces guest anxiety and questions before arrival?

### Takeaway
Independent, quantified research is thin. Vendor sources agree on giving the essentials early, repeating them across messages, sending a pre-arrival message with the check-in steps, and using photos and video. Vendor case studies show a majority of guests will use a pre-arrival web flow if it is easy, and that unclear multi-step flows get abandoned partway.

### Cited Findings
- Touch Stay Guest Communication Study: 2,024 vacation-rental guests surveyed about communication before, during and after the stay. The detailed figures are behind a download, and its date is unconfirmed. — [Touch Stay](https://touchstay.com/blog/best-guest-communication-tactics-between-booking-and-arrival)
- Touch Stay tactics: plan the flow as a welcome email, then a follow-up with key details, then a pre-arrival message with check-in instructions; repeat essential information so guests don't ask again; share the guidebook early; send door codes separately. — [Touch Stay](https://touchstay.com/blog/best-guest-communication-tactics-between-booking-and-arrival)
- A third-party glossary says hosts "commonly report a 30-50% reduction in routine guest inquiries" after adding a digital guidebook. This is unsourced and not traceable to primary data. — [Hostaway glossary](https://www.hostaway.com/glossary/digital-guidebook/)
- Canary customer review: 75% of arrivals used pre-registration, which cut check-in by up to 5 minutes per guest. But "some guests do not realize that the process involves multiple steps, with many completing only the first step." — [HotelTechReport, Canary review](https://hoteltechreport.com/reviews/q39wv19knwv5)
- Duve case studies (self-reported): online check-in at Novotel Geelong 58%, ULIV 86% (up from 50% on a previous tool), Pullman Phuket Panwa about 40%. Guest-app sessions: 2.5 per reservation (Pullman) and 4.8 (ULIV). — [Duve ULIV case study PDF](https://duve.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Case-study-ULIV-2025.pdf); [Duve](https://www.duve.com)
- Center Parcs redesigned its pre-arrival planning journey (check-in, adding attendees), tested with moderated and remote testing, and saw pre-arrival check-in completion rise "significantly". No figure was given. — [Atomic Media, Center Parcs](https://www.atomicmedia.co.uk/?p=1584)
- Boston University hospitality paper: hotels prepare answer templates for frequent pre-arrival questions (2021). — [BU BHR](https://www.bu.edu/bhr/files/2021/10/BHR_Kirillova_Lei_Wang_IM-in-Hotels_OCT.21.docx.pdf)

### Inferences
- What drives guest questions: "how do I get there / get in", "when can I arrive", "what do I still owe", "how do I reach you". A page that answers these four above the fold, in that order as arrival nears, covers most anxiety.
- The Canary finding (guests stop after step 1) argues against hiding essentials behind tabs or multi-step flows. A single scrolling page with clear sections is safer.
- Writing in the host's voice, adding a photo of the turn-off and repeating the arrival window in both the page and the confirmation message follows Touch Stay's "repeat essentials" advice.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed 2022-2026 study quantifying the drop in anxiety or questions from pre-arrival pages.
- Touch Stay's study figures were not accessible without downloading the full study.
